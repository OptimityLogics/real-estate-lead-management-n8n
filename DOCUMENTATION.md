# Real Estate Management

Workflow documentation for the n8n WhatsApp assistant. The workflow receives messages, loads property and FAQ data from Google Sheets, uses Gemini with six sheet tools for site visits and complaints, and replies on WhatsApp.

Configure credentials and spreadsheet placeholders after importing the workflow. Visit hours: Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 6 PM. Do not commit live tokens or customer data.

Known items to review before production: no recorded test results in the source documentation, no staff alerts, possible slot conflicts during simultaneous bookings, and rescheduling field names should be checked against the sheet columns.
